== Fragility notes ==
- Defined after results; small n; (a) uses only items above both thresholds.
- A uniform drift toward 'Yes' in Korean looks like 'toward Korea' whenever g > 0 dominates;
  read (a) with (c) and the sign(g) x sign(delta) table.
- justif10: almost all public mass is at 1-3, so the 1-5|6-10 split makes KR_A and US_A tiny; the split choice
  changes g materially (see sensitivity line).
- The Korean public answered the official Korean WVS questionnaire, whose wording differs in meaning from the
  English on some items (e.g. wav_90 'without their knowledge' vs official 'without consent'; Q235 official
  'parliament and parties' vs 'parliament and elections'). The ko prompts partly follow the official Korean
  (official wording where meaning matched, English-faithful where it did not, e.g. wav_92/93), so delta mixes
  language with translation-meaning shift. On wav_90 the ko prompt and the KR public share '동의 없이'
  while the en prompt says 'knowledge', so wording favours 'toward' there by construction.
- Hedging: where p_en sits near 0 or 1, the only room for |delta| >= 0.05 is toward 0.5; if that direction
  coincides with sign(g), 'toward Korea' cannot be told apart from 'less extreme in Korean' (see hedging check).
- Items are not independent: they come in WVS batteries (see battery clustering line); the binomial p and
  bootstrap CIs treat items as independent and so overstate precision.
- Two-option collapse discards intensity; 'Neither' handling is a choice (see sensitivity).
